Engram

A personal study planner for macOS.

Engram plans study time around a university timetable: lectures, write-ups, assignments and a dissertation, each a block on a calendar that ends as done, partly done or skipped. It runs on one Mac for one person. There is no account and no server.

Google Calendar

Engram can show its plan on the user’s phone by copying it into a Google calendar. It creates one calendar of its own and only ever reads or writes that calendar: it asks for Google’s calendar.app.created permission and nothing broader. The copy is one way; nothing is read from the user’s other calendars.
